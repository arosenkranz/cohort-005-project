# Cadence

Cadence is an online course platform where learners buy courses and instructors teach them. This glossary defines the language used in code, documentation, and what learners see, starting with how a learner gets their money back.

## Cancellation

**Cancellation**:
A learner's request to undo a course purchase and get their money back. It is a request, not an outcome: the purchase is not undone until staff approve it.
_Avoid_: Refund request, Reversal, Chargeback, "money back"

**Cancellation status**:
Where a Cancellation stands: **Requested** (waiting on staff), **Approved** (staff agreed, so a Refund is owed and the learner's Access ends), or **Declined** (staff refused; the Purchase and the learner's Access stay as they were). A learner cannot withdraw a Requested Cancellation.
_Avoid_: Pending (use Requested), Rejected (use Declined)

**Cancellation window**:
The 14 calendar days after purchase during which a Cancellation can be requested. The day of purchase counts as day one, and the learner gets the whole of the last day. Buying the same course again starts a new window.
_Avoid_: Refund window, Grace period

**Cancellation eligibility**:
The rules that decide whether a learner may request a Cancellation. All of these must hold:
- The request is inside the Cancellation window.
- Less than half of the course's lessons are completed.
- Less than 2 hours of the course have been watched.
- The purchase has no Claimed seat.
- The purchase has no open Cancellation (status Requested).

At exactly the limit (half the lessons, or 2 hours), a Cancellation is not allowed.
_Avoid_: Refund policy, Refund rules

**Cancellation reason**:
The learner's own explanation of why they want to cancel, written when they request a Cancellation.
_Avoid_: Note, Feedback

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner once a Cancellation is Approved. A Refund only exists as a result of an approved Cancellation.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund amount**:
What the learner actually paid for the purchase, never the course's list price. A learner who paid a reduced regional price gets back that reduced price.
_Avoid_: Course price, Refundable amount

**Chargeback**:
A dispute started by a learner's bank or card issuer, outside our own Cancellation process. The word is not used for Cancellations, even though the app has called them that.
_Avoid_: Using this word for a Cancellation

## Purchases and seats

**Purchase**:
A learner buying a course, recorded with the amount they paid. A Cancellation is always about one Purchase.
_Avoid_: Order, Transaction

**Access**:
A learner's ability to take a course's lessons, granted by a Purchase. An Approved Cancellation ends it.
_Avoid_: Enrolment, Subscription

**Claimed seat**:
A course place that came from a team licence and has been taken up by a team member. A Claimed seat belongs to the team, so the learner cannot cancel it on their own.
_Avoid_: Redeemed coupon, Used seat

**Staff**:
The people at Cadence who review Cancellations and decide whether to approve or decline them. No specific role is defined for this yet.
_Avoid_: Admin (unless meaning the admin area), Support agent
